How It Works

01Let's get in touch on a quick
call to discuss your business goals,
target audience, and required feature.

02 Before finalizing the
code I'll build a live,
interactive prototype of your
website's main layout directly
in the browser. This allows
you to click around,
view the layout and see
exactly how your branding,
color, and text content flow.

03 I bring the approved layout to life by coding it from
scratch using HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

04 I run your completed websites through testing
accross multiple devices and also increasing
maximum loading speed.
